# SignalOps

SignalOps is a modern company intelligence and operational monitoring dashboard designed to help organizations understand company health, employee workload, operational risks, alerts, and team performance from a single interface.

The project focuses on presenting organizational intelligence through a clean, responsive dashboard with interactive visualizations and structured risk insights.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📊 Company Dashboard
- Company health overview
- Employee and ticket metrics
- Overdue task monitoring
- Live operational alerts
- Organization-level KPIs

### 🛡️ Risk Map
- Department-level risk analysis
- Critical, High, Medium, and Low risk classification
- Risk severity indicators
- Active operational alerts
- Department risk visualization

### 👥 Team Intelligence
- Department performance insights
- Workload analysis
- Team health indicators
- Operational performance monitoring

### 📈 Analytics
- Company health trends
- Department risk charts
- Ticket activity
- Employee workload visualization
- Interactive data-driven UI

### 🤖 Recommendations
- Operational recommendations
- Priority-based actions
- Workload balancing suggestions
- Risk-based recommendations

### 📁 Upload Center
Designed to support organizational data ingestion and analysis.

### 📄 Reports
Structured reporting interface for company intelligence and operational insights.
